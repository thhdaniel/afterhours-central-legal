Privacy Policy — After Hours: Central

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This policy covers the mobile game After Hours: Central (Android package com.daniel.afterhourscentral3d), published by Daniel Tsang ("we", "the developer").

Summary

The game collects no personal data. It works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information. This includes your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, and usage data.

Data stored on your device

The game saves gameplay progress and settings (for example, the current mission, checkpoints and language choice) on your device only. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Advertising, analytics and third-party services

The game shows no advertising. It does not use analytics or crash reporting, and it includes no third-party SDKs that collect data. The game does not need an internet connection to play.

Accounts and purchases

The game does not need an account or sign-in. It has no in-app purchases.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or other personal information.

Children's privacy

We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
